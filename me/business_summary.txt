FARAYA LODGE - BUSINESS SUMMARY

Faraya Lodge is a small, family-run mountain guesthouse in Faraya, in the Keserwan mountains of Mount Lebanon, about an hour's drive from Beirut. It was opened in December 2022 by siblings Rami and Maya Haddad, who turned their family's old stone mountain house into an 8-room lodge. Our mission is simple: give every guest a warm, comfortable home in the mountains, with real Lebanese hospitality, honest prices, and easy access to the best of Faraya in every season.

ROOMS (prices per night, breakfast included):
- Standard Double Room (4 rooms, up to 2 guests): $70
- Mountain-View Suite (2 suites, up to 2 guests, private balcony and fireplace): $95
- Family Room (2 rooms, up to 4 guests): $120
Children under 6 stay free in their parents' room. Prices are in US dollars and are the same all year.

SERVICES AND EXTRAS:
- Breakfast (included): a Lebanese breakfast every morning from 7:30 to 10:30 AM, with man'oushe, labneh, cheese, eggs, fresh vegetables and fruit.
- Dinner: $18 per person, a home-style set menu cooked by Maya. Must be booked by 4 PM the same day.
- Winter: ski shuttle to the Mzaar slopes ($10 per person per day), ski and snowboard equipment rental through our partner shop ($20 per day), guided snowshoeing outing with Karim ($15 per person), and fireplace evenings in the lounge (free).
- Summer: guided mountain hike with Karim ($15 per person), half-day tour of the Faqra Roman ruins and the Natural Bridge (Jisr el Hajar) ($25 per person), and stargazing evenings on the terrace (free).
- Free Wi-Fi, free parking, and central heating in every room.

AMENITIES AND FACILITIES:
- In every room: private bathroom with 24-hour hot water, towels, shampoo and soap, a hairdryer, central heating, free Wi-Fi, an electric kettle with tea and coffee, and a small fridge. There is no TV in the rooms.
- Kitchen: the rooms do not have their own kitchen or stove. Guests can use a shared guest kitchenette on the ground floor, which has a gas stove, a microwave, a fridge, pots, pans and dishes, from 8 AM to 10 PM.
- Lounge: a shared lounge with a wood-burning fireplace, board games and books.
- Electricity: 24-hour electricity, with a backup generator and solar panels, so there are no power cuts.
- Laundry: a laundry service is available for $8 per load.
- Families: a baby crib can be added to any room for free, on request.
- Accessibility: two of the Standard Double rooms are on the ground floor, with no stairs.
- Parking: free private parking for guests.
- Getting there: the lodge does not offer airport transfers. Most guests drive up from Beirut.

TEAM:
- Rami Haddad, co-founder: runs operations, bookings and partnerships. He spent eight years in hotel management in Beirut before opening the lodge.
- Maya Haddad, co-founder: runs the guest experience and the kitchen. She is a trained chef and designed the breakfast and dinner menus.
- Karim, mountain guide: a certified ski instructor who grew up in Faraya and leads all hikes, snowshoeing outings and tours.

POLICIES:
Check-in from 2 PM, check-out by 11 AM. A 30% deposit confirms a booking; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (USD) or by card. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival; after that the deposit is not refunded. The lodge is open all year. Pets are not allowed. The whole lodge is non-smoking indoors.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT:
Faraya Lodge is small on purpose. With only 8 rooms, the people who greet you are the owners themselves. We combine a cozy family home with everything a mountain trip needs in one place: rooms, home cooking, a ski shuttle, and a local guide. Our prices are clear and stated in dollars, with no hidden fees.

HOW TO BOOK:
Our virtual host, Nadine, can answer questions and take your name, email and travel dates. Rami then contacts you by email within 24 hours to confirm availability and the deposit. Nadine cannot see live room availability or confirm bookings herself.
